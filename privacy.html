Privacy Policy

Metronome + for iPhone · Effective 13 October 2026

Summary

Metronome + does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app records or saves stays on your iPhone.

Microphone

  • The app uses the microphone only while you record a rhythm check with the Microphone input, after you allow access.
  • The audio is analysed on your iPhone to find when each note starts. The recording itself is not saved and is never uploaded.
  • What is saved with the result: the note times, the score, and a coarse loudness outline (100 values per second) used to draw the waveform. These cannot be played back as audio.

Uploaded recordings

If you choose an audio file to analyse, the app reads it on your iPhone. The file is not copied, kept or uploaded; only the same analysis results as above are saved.

Data stored on your iPhone

  • Settings (tempo, time signature, sound, language, latency offset), presets and your analysis history are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone.
  • Delete history at any time with History › Clear history. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
  • Your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data, under Apple’s terms.

Purchases

The Pro upgrade is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only receives confirmation that Pro is unlocked. We never see your name, email or payment details.

No tracking, ads or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites and makes no network connections of its own; the only connection is to the App Store for purchases.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the app is suitable for all ages.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

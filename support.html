Onset Support

A metronome for iPhone that measures how early or late each note is.

Contact

Email metronomeplus.help@protonmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

My taps all count as late

Bluetooth headphones and speakers delay the click by tens of milliseconds. Open Settings › Calibrate and tap along to 12 clicks with the headphones or speaker you play with. The app stores the offset and applies it to every check.

The microphone check finds too few notes

Play louder or put the iPhone closer to the instrument. Percussive and plucked sounds (drums, piano, guitar, claps) are detected most reliably. Wear headphones so the click isn’t recorded.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone

Pro is a one-time purchase linked to your Apple Account. Open Settings › See Pro › Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account.
